One earlobe appears attached and the other hangs freely. This is an observation, not a genotype.

PART 1 · PLENARY

Exit ticket · explain the pattern

1 · Two carrier parents Aa have a child. What is the chance of albinism (aa)?

25% (1 in 4) for each pregnancy; each pregnancy has the same probability.

2 · What phenotype ratio is expected if A is dominant?

Three with the dominant phenotype to one with the recessive phenotype (3:1).

3 · Match the terms: genotype, phenotype, heterozygous and recessive allele.

Allele combination; observable features; two different alleles; expressed only without a dominant allele.

Before the advanced crosses

Check 1 · For Bb × Bb, what is the chance of bb?

25% (one outcome out of four).

BB and Bb, if B is completely dominant.

Check 3 · What does a pedigree help you trace?

The inheritance of a characteristic through generations of a family.

PART 2 · PLENARY

Exit ticket · connect the patterns

1 · What genotype does a recessive tester have in a test cross?

Homozygous recessive, such as ee.

2 · A man XʳY and a woman XᴿXᴿ have children. What proportion of sons are colour-blind, and what genotype do daughters have?

0% of sons are colour-blind; daughters are XᴿXʳ carriers because each receives the father’s Xʳ.

3 · What makes the AB blood group an example of codominance?

Both Iᴬ and Iᴮ alleles are expressed in the phenotype.
