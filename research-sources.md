# Section 17.4 syllabus audit and video verification

## Syllabus audit

The official source is the [Cambridge IGCSE Biology 0610 syllabus for 2026–2028](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/697203-2026-2028-syllabus.pdf), section 17.4, pages 39–40 [1]. All 18 listed outcomes were independently assessed against the website's explanations, examples, diagrams, questions and interactive calculations.

The audit found two points needing a correction. Core point 2 covered alleles and genotype examples but did not explicitly define genotype as an organism's genetic make-up. Its text now states the organism-level definition. Core point 10 explained pedigree symbols and inheritance clues but lacked a learner response task; an open-ended question now asks learners to infer genotypes from the displayed dominant pedigree. The site also now explicitly says that X-linked recessive characteristics can be more common in XY males, matching the full wording of Supplement point 16.

With these corrections, Core points 1–12 and Supplement points 13–18 are represented in syllabus order with teaching content and practice. The lesson labels these points Core and Extended (Supplement), preserving Cambridge's official term in the label. The independently checked point results were: Core 1, 3–9 and 11–12 complete; Core 2 and 10 corrected; Supplement 13–18 complete [1].

## Verified lesson videos

**Part 1 · Core points 11–12:** [IGCSE Biology – Monohybrid inheritance, topic 17.4](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v4y4l0Q6dJY) by IGBioComplete is described as a Cambridge IGCSE Biology 0610/0970 Chapter 17 topic 17.4 review for exams from 2027. Its chapter list places genetic diagrams at 3:21 and Punnett squares at 4:11, followed by pedigree diagrams at 5:17. The embedded excerpt begins at 3:21 and ends at 5:17, keeping it with the Part 1 cross and Punnett-square objectives [2].

**Part 2 · Supplement points 13–18:** [17. Inheritance, Part 2 (2/2), Cambridge IGCSE Biology 0610 for exams in 2026, 2027 and 2028](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IHkShmHVdpk) by IGCSE Study Buddy was assessed against the Supplement objectives. The relevant chapters cover test crosses from 12:09–16:07, codominance and ABO groups from 17:00–20:11, and sex linkage/colour blindness from 20:12–23:45. The embed starts at 12:09 and ends at 23:45 to skip the introductory Core review and concentrate on the Part 2 content [3].

The two video pages and their chapter/transcript details were opened, and their relevant lesson content was analyzed before selection [2] [3]. The original Core slide link, [Monohybrid Crosses & Punnett Squares Explained](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vzBer5coUzg), teaches a valid 3:1 monohybrid example but is presented for AP/IB rather than Cambridge IGCSE; it was replaced by the exact 0610 topic 17.4 review [4]. The original Supplement slide link, [Red-Green Color Blindness – Tales from the Genome](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1ZX12WbmLxA), is a short pedigree-analysis prompt and does not teach the required sex-linked genetic diagram and cross, so the Cambridge IGCSE Part 2 lesson was selected instead [5].
